## Report serious online content

A secure reporting page for content such as terrorism, child exploitation, violence and threats, threats to state security, blackmail and fraud, and hate speech.

- Send the **link** and a written description. We never ask for photos, videos or the content itself.
- Receive a **reference number** (`RP-…`) and a PDF copy, and follow the status and stages of your report at any time from the homepage.
- Reports are reviewed confidentially, and serious cases are referred through official channels.

VYROXX IT is a private company, not a government body, and this page does not replace an official complaint. You can also report directly on the official DGSN platform at https://www.e-blagh.ma. In immediate danger, call the Police (19) or the Royal Gendarmerie (177).
